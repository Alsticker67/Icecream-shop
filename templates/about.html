About Us

Scooping happiness in Mumbai since 2015.

Treat & Tasty Ice Cream started as a small parlour in Mahape with just six flavours and a simple idea: real ice cream made fresh every morning from milk, cream and seasonal fruit. Today we churn more than thirty flavours, from classic vanilla and rich Belgian chocolate to mango, sitaphal and our famous softy.

Whether you drop by for a single cone or order a family pack for a celebration, every scoop is made with the same care. No artificial colours, no shortcuts, just honest ice cream.
